About Find My Ride

Empowering Safe & Smart Campus Transit

Designed specifically for university campuses to eliminate late-night transit friction and build a seamless, student-friendly mobility network.

🛡️
Campus Security

100% University Verified

Every student and driver is validated through institutional university credentials. External unverified parties are strictly prohibited from booking or accepting rides.

🌙
24/7 Safety

Night-Owl Safe Escort

Studying late at the Central Library, Computer Labs, or Innovation Hub? Request safe escort shuttles direct to your campus residence doorstep.

Live Leaflet Geo-Tracking

Live driver telemetry streamed directly to your screen eliminates guesswork. Monitor real-time approach angles, live distances, and ETA markers.
